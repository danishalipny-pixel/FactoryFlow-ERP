# FactoryFlow ERP — REST API Reference

The FactoryFlow ERP backend exposes a secure, JSON-based REST API designed for high-performance industrial operations.

**Base URL**: `http://localhost:5001/api`  
**Authentication**: Bearer Token (JWT) in `Authorization` header  
**Default Timezone**: `Africa/Cairo`  
**Default Currency**: `EGP`

---

## 1. Authentication & System Health

### `GET /api/health`
Verifies server status, operating jurisdiction, and company profile.
- **Access**: Public
- **Response `200 OK`**:
```json
{
  "status": "ONLINE",
  "system": "FACTORYFLOW ERP - Egypt MTO Manufacturing System",
  "timezone": "Africa/Cairo",
  "country": "مصر",
  "company": {
    "nameAr": "شركة الأهرام للصناعات الهندسية والمعدنية ش.م.م",
    "nameEn": "Al-Ahram Engineering & Metal Industries S.A.E.",
    "taxRegistrationNo": "100-245-892",
    "country": "مصر"
  },
  "timestamp": "2026-11-06T16:00:00.000Z"

```

### `POST /api/auth/login`
Authenticates a user and returns a signed JWT.
- **Request Body**:
```json
{
  "email": "admin@factoryflow.eg",
  "password": "FactoryFlow@2026"
}
```
- **Response `200 OK`**:
```json
{
  "token": "eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9...",
  "user": {
    "id": "uuid",
```

### `POST /api/auth/switch-role`
Allows rapid demonstration switching between any of the 11 enterprise manufacturing roles.
- **Request Body**: `{"targetRole": "PRODUCTION_MANAGER"}`

## 3. CRM & Sales (MTO Core)

### `GET /api/sales/customers`
Retrieves registered Egyptian business customers.

### `POST /api/sales/customers`
Registers a new customer account with Egyptian Tax ID and Commercial Registry.
- **Request Body**:
```json
{
  "nameAr": "شركة السويدي للأنظمة الكهربائية ش.م.م",
  "nameEn": "Elsewedy Electrical Systems S.A.E.",
  "taxRegistrationNo": "100-988-341",
  "commercialRegNo": "78412 - العاشر من رمضان",
  "contactPerson": "المهندس كريم الشريف",
  "email": "procurement@elsewedy-systems.eg",
  "phone": "+20 100 234 5678",
  "address": "المنطقة الصناعية الأولى، العاشر من رمضان",
  "city": "العاشر من رمضان",
  "governorate": "الشرقية",
  "paymentTermsDays": 45,
  "creditLimitEgp": 1500000
}
```

### `POST /api/sales/quotations`
Generates a formal engineering quotation with BOM cost estimation and statutory 14% Egyptian VAT.

### `POST /api/sales/orders` *(MTO Core Engine Trigger)*
Confirms a sales order and executes the atomic MTO lifecycle in a single database transaction:
1. Validates customer contract.
2. Explodes multi-level BOM.
3. Checks warehouse inventory.
4. Locks reserved materials for the order.
5. Emits automatic purchase requests for material shortages.
6. Dispatches linked production orders (`PO-MTO-...`) with complete customer order traceability.

## 4. Engineering & Products

### `GET /api/engineering/products`
Lists raw materials, sub-assemblies, and finished products.

### `GET /api/engineering/boms`
Retrieves multi-level Bills of Materials with item scrap percentages and yield rates.

### `GET /api/engineering/work-centers`
Lists factory machines, daily operating hours, and hourly depreciation/operating rates in EGP.

---

## 5. MTO Production & Job Cards

### `GET /api/production/orders`
Lists all active production orders with priority, completion percentage, and deadlines.

### `POST /api/production/operations/:id/update-status`
Updates routing job card status (`IN_PROGRESS`, `COMPLETED`), logging actual setup/run hours, good output, and scrap.

### `POST /api/production/orders/:id/issue-material`
Issues reserved raw materials to the production order, deducting from warehouse inventory without negative stock.

### `POST /api/production/orders/:id/complete`
Marks fabrication complete and transfers units to final dispatch quality inspection.

---

## 6. Inventory & Warehousing

### `GET /api/inventory/stock`
Returns current stock on hand, reserved quantities, and available quantities per warehouse bin.

### `GET /api/inventory/reservations`
Lists order-specific raw material reservations locking stock per sales order.

---

## 7. Procurement & Purchasing

### `GET /api/procurement/requests`
Lists shortage purchase requests automatically generated by the MTO engine.

### `POST /api/procurement/orders`
Issues a supplier purchase order with statutory 14% Egyptian VAT and 1% Withholding Tax (`T4`).

### `POST /api/procurement/goods-receipt`
Logs a Goods Receipt Note (GRN) with lot/batch tracking, updating inventory balances.

---

## 8. Quality Assurance (QA/QC)

### `GET /api/quality/inspections`
Retrieves incoming, in-process, and final inspection logs.

### `POST /api/quality/inspections`
Records physical inspection results, measured tolerances, and defect severity (`PASSED`, `REWORK_REQUIRED`, `REJECTED`).

---

## 9. Finance, Costing & ETA E-Invoicing

### `POST /api/finance/invoices`
Generates a formal customer tax invoice with 14% VAT, 1% WHT, and cryptographic UUID.

### `GET /api/finance/invoices/:id/eta-payload`
Generates the complete JSON document required by the Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA) Document Type Version 1.0 standard.

### `POST /api/finance/invoices/:id/submit-eta`
Validates and marks invoice status as `Valid` on the Egyptian Tax Authority e-invoicing portal.

### `POST /api/finance/payments`
Records customer payments against invoices (Cash, Cheque, Bank Transfer), updating invoice status to `PAID`.

### `GET /api/finance/order-costing/:productionOrderId`
Calculates the true Cost of Goods Sold (Actual Materials + Direct Labor + Machine Overhead) and net profit margin for a customer order.
